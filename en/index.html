Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Echo runs entirely on your Mac, even in airplane mode. You only need internet to download it.

What happens to my voice and my text?

They stay on your Mac. Echo sends no audio or text to any server, has no accounts and doesn't use the cloud. You can delete your history whenever you want.

Does it work in any app?

Yes: anywhere you can type with the keyboard. Echo inserts the text at your cursor.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Spanish, and it detects automatically which one you're speaking. It works best when each sentence is in a single language; if you mix both within the same sentence, it sometimes translates part of it.

Which Mac do I need?

Any Mac running macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, with an Intel or Apple Silicon processor (M1, M2, M3, M4). It's even faster on Apple Silicon Macs.

How do I install it?

Open the downloaded file and drag Echo to Applications. If macOS says it can't verify the app, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. First time only. When you open it, Echo walks you through granting the permissions step by step.

How do I buy it?

Tap Buy and pay with PayPal or a card: your license key shows right away (the checkout page is in Spanish; PayPal shows in your language). It's a one-time payment ($25 launch price, then $35): paste the key into Echo › License and you're done. It's yours for good, with Echo 1.x updates included. No subscription.

Can I use it on several Macs?

Each license is for one Mac. If you switch computers, you can deactivate it on the old one and activate it on the new one.
