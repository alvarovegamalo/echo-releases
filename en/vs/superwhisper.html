Echo vs superwhisper (2026): offline dictation for Mac compared

Both are good Mac dictation apps. They differ on price model, privacy, languages and platforms. Here is where each one fits, including where superwhisper is the better choice.

Who should pick which

Pick Echo if…

  • you want to pay once ($25 launch price, then $35) instead of subscribing
  • your voice must stay on your Mac, with no cloud
  • you dictate in Spanish and English, sometimes in the same sentence
  • you have an Intel Mac (Echo runs on Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13+)
  • you want a Spanish-first interface

Pick superwhisper if…

  • you also use Windows or iPhone
  • you want to choose between several models, local or cloud
  • you need translation, file transcription or your own API keys
  • a free tier with small local models is enough to start
  • you want a refund window: it has a 30-day refund

Price: one-time vs subscription

superwhisper Pro is $8.49 a month, $84.99 a year or $249.99 once. Echo is $25 once during launch, then $35. If you will dictate for years, a one-time price costs far less over time. If you prefer a low monthly fee or need Pro features, superwhisper's subscription also makes sense. superwhisper also has a free tier with small local models, handy for trying it out.

Privacy and working offline

Echo processes everything on your Mac: your voice never leaves the computer and it works with no connection, on a plane or on bad Wi-Fi. superwhisper can also run local models. Its Pro plan adds cloud models and your own API keys, so check which mode you use if privacy is your priority.

Spanish, English and mixed sentences

Echo is made in Ecuador and its interface is Spanish-first. It dictates in Spanish, in English, and in both inside one sentence, which is common if you work with technical terms or with clients in two languages. You can add custom vocabulary for names and acronyms.

Where superwhisper is better

To be clear: superwhisper has more platforms (Windows and iOS), more model options, translation, file transcription and your own API keys. If you need any of these, or want to dictate on an iPhone, superwhisper covers more ground than Echo. Echo is a smaller app made by one person, focused on doing Mac dictation well.

How to try Echo

Download the app and use it for 14 days with no card. If you like it, you buy it on the site with PayPal or a card and get your key right away. You can install both apps and compare them with your own voice and your own apps. That is the best test.
