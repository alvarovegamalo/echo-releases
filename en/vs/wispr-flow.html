Privacy: local vs cloud

This is the biggest difference. Wispr Flow processes audio in the cloud, so it needs internet and your voice travels to its servers. Echo does everything on your Mac: nothing leaves the computer and it works with no connection. If you dictate client emails, medical notes, legal text, or simply prefer not to send your voice to a third party, that matters.

Long-term price

Wispr Flow Pro is $15 a month, or $12 a month if you pay for the year ($144 a year), and there is no lifetime option. Echo is $25 once during launch, then $35, with no subscription. Over two years of use the gap is large. Wispr Flow has a free tier of 2,000 words a week on desktop and 50% off for students, which can be the better deal if you dictate little or you are studying.

Dictating in Spanish and English

Echo is Spanish-first and handles sentences that mix both languages, as often happens in daily work. It also has custom vocabulary for names, brands and acronyms, plus meeting transcription. It types into any Mac app where your cursor is.

Where Wispr Flow is better

Wispr Flow reaches more places: Mac, Windows, iOS and Android. If you dictate on your phone or work on a Windows PC, Echo will not help, because it exists only for Mac. Echo is made by one developer in Cuenca, Ecuador, and focuses on one job: local dictation on Mac.
